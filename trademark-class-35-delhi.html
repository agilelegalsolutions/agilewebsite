Why Class 35 is the most filed class in Delhi

Class 35 is the primary commercial service class under the Nice Classification. It protects business operations such as retail stores, wholesale trading, franchise networks, advertising, and online e-commerce platforms. As a major trading and commercial capital, Delhi NCR files more Class 35 applications than any other service category.

1. Scope of Trademark Class 35 Services

Under the official classification system administered by the Trade Marks Registry in Dwarka, Class 35 encompasses services related to:

Retail & Wholesale Outlets

Bringing together a variety of goods enabling consumers to conveniently view and purchase products in brick-and-mortar retail stores or distribution networks.

E-Commerce & Online Portals

Operating digital shopping portals, multi-vendor electronic marketplaces, and online catalog ordering services for commercial enterprises.

2. Crucial Distinction: Class 35 vs Manufacturing Classes

A frequent point of confusion among Delhi business owners is the difference between product manufacturing classes (Classes 1 to 34) and service trading in Class 35:

Category Manufacturing Class Class 35 Retail / Trading
Clothing & Fashion Class 25: Protects readymade garments and footwear manufactured under your brand Class 35: Protects the retail boutique, clothing showroom, or e-commerce fashion store
Cosmetics & Skincare Class 3: Protects manufactured perfumes, soaps, and beauty formulations Class 35: Protects the beauty supply store, distributor, or online cosmetic website
Pharmaceuticals Class 5: Protects manufactured medicines, syrups, and supplements Class 35: Protects wholesale medicine distribution, retail pharmacies, and chemist outlets

Recommendation: If you manufacture products AND operate your own branded retail stores or online shop, our advocates advise dual filing across both classes to achieve complete brand defense.

3. Statutory Government Fees for Class 35 in Delhi

Official fees for Form TM-A e-filing at the Trade Marks Registry (Boudhik Sampada Bhawan, Dwarka) are set by the Trade Marks Rules, 2017:

  • Individuals, Sole Proprietorships & MSMEs: Rs 4,500 per class (requires valid Udyam Registration Certificate).
  • Partnerships, LLPs & Private Limited Companies: Rs 9,000 per class without MSME concession.

For an exhaustive breakdown of official fees, visit our trademark registration Delhi page.

4. Best Practices for Drafting Class 35 Descriptions

The Trade Marks Registry routinely raises objections if Class 35 specifications are drafted too vaguely. To ensure smooth acceptance:

  1. Avoid General Terms: Avoid blanket phrases like "all retail and wholesale services".
  2. Specify Product Verticals: Accurately identify product categories, such as "retail and wholesale services connected with the sale of readymade garments, hosiery, and fashion accessories".
  3. Cross-Class Search: Before filing, conduct a search via our trademark search guide to verify no conflicting marks exist in related manufacturing classes.

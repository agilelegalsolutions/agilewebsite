Why monitoring your trademark status is crucial

After filing Form TM-A, your application passes through strict statutory checkpoints under the Trade Marks Act, 1999. Missing a statutory deadline (such as the 30-day objection response window or the 2-month counter-statement timeline) can cause your application to be permanently abandoned.

2. Detailed Next Steps for High-Risk Statuses

Status: "Objected"

Over 65% of all applications receive an objection. It is not a refusal. You must download the Examination Report and draft a legal reply addressing:

  • Section 9: Proving acquired distinctiveness via Rule 25 commercial use affidavits.
  • Section 11: Demonstrating visual, phonetic, and conceptual differences with cited marks.

Status: "Formalities Chk Fail"

This status occurs when filing particulars contain clerical errors: incorrect NICE class specification, missing translation for non-English words, or non-filing of user affidavit. A legal response must be filed to avoid the application being treated as abandoned.

Status: "Ready for Show Cause Hearing"

Hearings for Delhi jurisdiction are conducted by Hearing Officers at Boudhik Sampada Bhawan, Dwarka via Cisco Webex. An enrolled advocate submits written arguments beforehand and presents oral arguments during the video conference.

Frequently Asked Questions: Trademark Application Status

The Objected status means the Trade Marks Registry examiner has issued an Examination Report raising statutory objections under Section 9 or Section 11 of the Trade Marks Act, 1999. The applicant must file a formal written legal reply within exactly 30 days from receiving the examination report.

Formalities Chk Fail indicates clerical or documentation discrepancies in your initial Form TM-A filing, such as a missing Power of Attorney, blurry logo image, or incorrect translation. You must file a corrective response or Form TM-M with the supporting document to rectify the formality issue.

Send to Vienna Codification is an administrative internal process where the Registry assigns international numerical codes to visual elements, logos, or figurative designs under the Vienna Agreement. It is a standard procedure and requires no action from the applicant.

Advertised before acceptance means the trademark has been published in the official Trade Marks Journal to invite public opposition. Third parties have a statutory window of 4 months from publication to file an opposition under Section 21.

An Opposed status remains active throughout adversarial opposition proceedings, which typically span 12 to 24 months. The applicant must file a formal Counter-Statement on Form TM-O within 2 months of receiving the Notice of Opposition, followed by evidence affidavits.

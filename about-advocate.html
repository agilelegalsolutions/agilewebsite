Advocate Arun Kumar Jha

Principal IP Attorney & Trademark Advocate • Agile Legal Solutions®

Advocate Arun Kumar Jha is a practicing Advocate and Intellectual Property Attorney based in Rohini, New Delhi. With extensive experience before the Trade Marks Registry (Delhi Jurisdiction), Intellectual Property Appellate bodies, and judicial courts, his practice focuses on brand protection, trademark prosecution, objection drafting under Sections 9 & 11 of the Trade Marks Act 1999, show-cause hearing advocacy, opposition proceedings, and comprehensive IP advisory for businesses across India.

Core Practice Areas

Structured legal assistance across all stages of intellectual property lifecycle in India.

Trademark Search & Filing

Prior public search on IP India database, Nice classification analysis across 45 classes, and compliant Form TM-A electronic filing before the Trade Marks Registry.

Objection Reply & Hearings

Statutory legal replies to Examination Reports under Section 9 (distinctiveness) and Section 11 (conflict) within 30 days, followed by oral hearing representation.

Opposition & IP Disputes

Filing and defending Notices of Opposition (Form TM-O), counter-statements, evidence affidavits under Rule 45-47, and trademark rectification proceedings.

Copyright Protection

Registration and statutory defense for literary works, artistic creations, software code, musical compositions, and cinematograph films under the Copyright Act 1957.

Industrial Design & Patents

Design registrations under Designs Act 2000 for novel aesthetic shapes, packaging, and ornamental patterns; preliminary patent drafting and filing support.

TM Renewal & Portfolio Management

Periodic decennial renewals (Form TM-R), restoration of abandoned marks, trademark licensing agreements, and assignment deed drafting (Form TM-P).

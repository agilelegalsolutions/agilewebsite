Why Class 25 is fiercely contested in Delhi

As North India's largest garment manufacturing and trading hub, Delhi registers tens of thousands of clothing trademarks annually. Because thousands of brand names already exist on the Register, Class 25 applications face frequent Section 11 conflict objections and third-party oppositions. Rigorous brand clearance search before filing is essential.

1. Scope of Goods Under Trademark Class 25

Under the Nice Classification system administered by the Trade Marks Registry in Dwarka, Class 25 covers three broad categories of apparel goods:

1. Clothing

Readymade garments, shirts, t-shirts, jeans, trousers, suits, ethnic wear, sarees, lehengas, sportswear, hosiery, undergarments, and uniforms.

2. Footwear

Shoes, casual sneakers, formal leather shoes, boots, slippers, sandals, sports footwear, socks, and shoe soles.

3. Headgear

Hats, caps, beanies, turbans, bandanas, visors, and protective head coverings.

2. Crucial Strategy: Class 25 (Apparel) vs Class 35 (Retail Store)

Fashion entrepreneurs often confuse manufacturing protection with retail protection:

  • Class 25 protects your brand name on the garment tag: It stops other manufacturers from stitching your brand name onto their shirts, jeans, or shoes.
  • Class 35 protects your boutique or e-commerce shop name: It protects your retail store identity, showroom signage, or online shopping domain. Learn more in our trademark Class 35 Delhi guide.

If you sell your own manufactured clothing line through your own branded retail stores or website, our advocates strongly advise filing in both Class 25 and Class 35.

3. How to Clear a Clothing Brand Name in Delhi

Due to high crowding in Class 25, our practice (established in 2017) applies rigorous clearance protocols before filing:

  1. Phonetic Similarity Search: Assessing whether your proposed name sounds identical or similar to registered brands (e.g., "Kool" vs "Cool"). Use our trademark search India guide.
  2. Device Mark / Logo Scrutiny: Verifying that your emblem or graphical logo does not infringe earlier visual marks coded under Vienna Classification.
  3. Prior User Date (Rule 25): If you have already sold garments in wholesale markets like Gandhi Nagar, Tank Road, or Chandni Chowk, compiling oldest GST invoices and user affidavits to claim prior rights.

4. Statutory Government Fees for Class 25 in Delhi

Official filing fees at the Trade Marks Registry (Boudhik Sampada Bhawan, Dwarka) are set by statutory rules:

  • Individuals, Startups & MSMEs: Rs 4,500 per class (with valid Udyam Registration Certificate).
  • Companies & LLPs without MSME: Rs 9,000 per class.

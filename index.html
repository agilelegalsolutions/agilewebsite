Trade Mark & Trademark Registration FAQ

Find answers to essential questions about Trade Mark search, filing procedures, hiring a Trademark Registration Advocate in Delhi, and brand protection.

To register a Trade Mark in Delhi, our expert Trademark Advocates conduct an exhaustive trademark search across the Trade Marks Registry database, identify your brand's correct class (Classes 1 to 45), draft Form TM-A with required user affidavits, and file the online application. Upon filing, you can immediately begin using the ™ symbol.

A specialized Trademark Advocate in Delhi ensures your mark avoids common examination objections under Sections 9 & 11 of the Trade Marks Act, 1999, prepares legal replies to examination reports, represents you in show-cause hearings before the Registrar, and defends your mark during third-party opposition proceedings.

Once an application is submitted online, the ™ symbol can be used instantly. If no examination objections or oppositions are raised by the Trade Marks Registry, official registration certificates are issued within 6 to 12 months, granting exclusive rights to use the ® registered trademark symbol.

Essential documents include: Brand logo/wordmark, Applicant ID & Address proof (Aadhaar/PAN/GST certificate), MSME / Startup Certificate (for 50% govt fee concession), and Form TM-M (Power of Attorney) authorizing Agile Legal Solutions®.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about registering and protecting your trademark in India.

What is a trademark, and why is registration necessary?

A trademark is a distinctive visual sign, brand name, logo, or tagline that distinguishes your goods and services. Registering it grants you exclusive statutory monopoly across all 28 states and 8 union territories in India under the Trade Marks Act, 1999, preventing competitors from duplicating your identity.

Can I use the ™ symbol immediately after filing?

Yes! As soon as your TM-A application is submitted online and the official government filing receipt with your unique application number is generated (within 24 hours with Agile Legal Solutions), you are legally entitled to display the ™ symbol beside your brand name and logo.

How long does trademark registration take in India?

While you receive your application number and ™ rights within 24 hours, the full examination, advertisement in the official Trade Marks Journal, and final issuance of the 10-year official ® Registration Certificate generally takes 6 to 12 months if uncontested.

What are the government fees for trademark registration?

The official Government fees are categorized as follows:
  • ₹4,500 per class: For Individuals, Sole Proprietors, DPIIT-recognized Startups, and MSME/Udyam registered entities (50% fee concession).
  • ₹9,000 per class: For Private Limited Companies, LLPs, and Partnerships without MSME certificates.

What documents are required to file a trademark application?

Document requirements are minimal: (1) Brand name or logo image file, (2) Applicant Identity & Address proof (PAN card / Aadhaar / GST certificate), (3) MSME/Udyam Certificate if claiming the 50% discount, and (4) Signed Form TM-M (Power of Attorney) authorizing our advocate to file on your behalf.

What happens if my trademark application receives an objection?

If the Trade Marks Registry raises an examination report objection under Section 9 (lack of distinctiveness) or Section 11 (similar existing marks), our High Court advocates draft and file a comprehensive legal reply along with evidence of prior use. In our Growth and Enterprise packages, initial objection reply drafting is fully included.

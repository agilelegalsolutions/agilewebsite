# Agile Legal Solutions

Law practice based in Rohini, New Delhi, led by Advocate Arun Kumar Jha. The practice focuses on intellectual property matters including trademark search, trademark application filing (Form TM-A), examination report objection replies, show cause hearings, opposition proceedings, renewal, copyright, patent and design filings, and trademark litigation advisory before the Trade Marks Registry and the High Court of Delhi. All information is provided strictly for informational purposes in compliance with Bar Council of India regulations and does not constitute legal advice or solicitation.

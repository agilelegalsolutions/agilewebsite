How to choose a trademark lawyer in Delhi

When evaluating a trademark advocate in Delhi, consider the following objective and neutral criteria:

  • 1. Bar Council enrolment: Verify that the counsel is an Advocate enrolled with the Bar Council of Delhi under the Advocates Act, 1961, ensuring authorized standing before registries and judicial forums.
  • 2. Registry hearing experience: Ensure the counsel regularly attends show-cause hearings before Hearing Officers at the Trade Marks Registry in Dwarka, ensuring knowledgeable procedural handling.
  • 3. Written scope and fees: A clear engagement should itemize statutory official filing fees separately from professional legal fees for drafting, filing, and hearing appearances.
  • 4. Class-selection advice: Your lawyer must identify appropriate classes under Nice Classification (Classes 1–45) to ensure sufficient coverage without unnecessary multi-class expenses.
  • 5. Direct communication: Work directly with the practicing advocate handling your submissions rather than unassisted digital portals or customer support intermediaries.
  • 6. Post-filing support: Confirm ongoing tracking of Trade Marks Journal advertisements, objection deadlines (30-day response window), and 10-year renewal reminders.

The five-step trademark registration process

Securing a registered trademark involves five sequential legal stages governed by the Trade Marks Rules, 2017:

1

Brand search & clearance analysis

The advocate examines proposed names, logos, or slogans across IP India databases to evaluate distinctiveness and avoid conflicting prior marks.

2

Form TM-A electronic filing

Application details, class specifications, and user affidavits are prepared and submitted electronically, generating an application number for immediate ™ use.

3

Registry examination & objection reply

If the Registry issues an Examination Report under Section 9 or 11, the advocate submits a formal legal counter-statement within 30 days and attends hearings.

4

Journal publication & opposition window

Accepted marks are published in the weekly Trade Marks Journal for 4 months, allowing third parties to file opposition notices under Section 21.

5

Registration certificate & ® symbol entitlement

If uncontested, the Registrar issues the Certificate of Registration, granting exclusive rights and permission to use the ® symbol for 10 years.

Note: Timelines vary depending on Registry examination schedules, third-party oppositions, and statutory hearing allocations.

Cookie Policy

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, enhance user experience, and provide web diagnostics.

2. How We Use Cookies

Agile Legal Solutions® uses minimal, essential cookies on our website:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for enabling navigation, secure form submission, and modal displays.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Helps us analyze website traffic anonymously to improve page load speed and content layout.

3. Third-Party Services

We may embed essential third-party scripts (such as Google Web Fonts, FontAwesome, and Web3Forms for contact submission) which may set technical cookies to ensure service delivery.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can choose to disable or block cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may impact certain interactive features on our website.

All trademark applications where the principal place of business is located in Delhi, Haryana, Punjab, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, and Chandigarh fall under the jurisdiction of the Trade Marks Registry, Dwarka, New Delhi.

Step-by-Step Trademark Registration Process in Delhi

Filing for trademark protection involves 5 systematic legal stages executed by our registered Trademark Advocates:

1

Comprehensive Trademark Search

Before filing, our advocates conduct a thorough search on the official IP India database across wordmarks, phonetics, and Vienna codes to confirm brand availability and ensure no conflicting prior marks exist.

Time: Within 2 to 4 Hours
2

Class Identification (Nice Classification 1–45)

Goods and services are categorized into 45 international classes (Classes 1–34 for manufacturing/goods, Classes 35–45 for commercial services). Selecting the correct class is crucial to prevent rejection.

Precision Legal Classification
3

Online Filing of Form TM-A & Immediate ™ Usage

Our advocates digitally sign and submit your application with the Trade Marks Registry. The moment Form TM-A is submitted, you receive your official government application number and can immediately affix the ™ symbol to your brand.

Immediate ™ Protection
4

Examination Report & Objection Management

The Trade Marks Registry examines the application. If an Examination Report is issued with objections under Section 9 (distinctiveness) or Section 11 (similarity), our advocates draft a comprehensive legal counter-response within 30 days and appear for show-cause hearings.

Statutory Response Period: 30 Days
5

Advertisement in Journal & Certificate Issuance (®)

Once accepted, the mark is published in the weekly Trade Marks Journal for 4 months for public scrutiny. If no opposition is filed, the Registrar issues the official Certificate of Registration, granting the ® registered symbol valid for 10 years (renewable indefinitely).

® Registered Status Granted

Government Fees for Trademark Registration in Delhi

Under the Trade Marks Rules, 2017, the official statutory fees for online filing (Form TM-A) are categorized based on applicant type:

Applicant Category Eligibility Criteria Govt. Filing Fee (Per Class)
Individual / Sole Proprietorship Individual business owners, professionals, freelancers ₹4,500
MSME / Udyam Registered Enterprise Micro, Small & Medium enterprises holding Udyam Certificate ₹4,500 (50% Subsidy)
DPIIT Recognized Startup Entities recognized by Startup India initiative ₹4,500 (50% Subsidy)
Other Entities (Pvt Ltd, LLP, Partnership) Companies without MSME/Startup registration ₹9,000

Documents Required for Trademark Filing in Delhi

To initiate your filing, the following documentation is required:

  • Applicant Identification: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Certificate of Incorporation.
  • Brand Details: High-resolution copy of your logo, wordmark, or brand name.
  • Date of First Use: If the mark is already in use, a notarized User Affidavit with invoices/bills showing earliest commercial use.
  • Power of Attorney (Form TM-M): Authorizing Agile Legal Solutions® advocates to represent your matter before the Registry.
  • MSME / Startup Certificate: If applicable, to claim the 50% government fee concession.

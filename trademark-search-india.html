A preliminary Trademark Public Search on the official portal of the Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (IP India) is the most critical first step before investing in brand building, domain registration, and government application fees.

The 3 Main Types of Trademark Searches on IP India

The government portal offers three search modalities:

1. Wordmark Search

Used to find exact or partial matches for a brand name or wordmark string. The search dropdown options include:

  • Contains: Searches for marks containing the search term anywhere in the string.
  • Starts With: Searches for marks starting with the prefix.
  • Match With: Exact string match.

2. Phonetic Search

Identifies marks that sound phonetically similar or identical to your proposed name, even if spelled differently (e.g., "Quick" vs "Kwik", "Cure" vs "Kure"). This prevents Section 11 deceptively similar objections.

3. Vienna Code Search (Device / Logo Marks)

Used for graphical devices, shapes, and logos according to the international Vienna Classification (e.g., Category 01 for celestial bodies, Category 03 for animals).

Overview of Trademark Classes (Nice Classification)

All commercial products and services are divided into 45 international classes:

Goods: Classes 1 to 34
  • Class 5: Pharmaceuticals & Medical
  • Class 9: Software, Electronics, Hardware
  • Class 25: Clothing, Footwear & Apparel
  • Class 30: Coffee, Tea, Spices & Food
Services: Classes 35 to 45
  • Class 35: Advertising, Retail & Business Mgmt
  • Class 41: Education, Training & Entertainment
  • Class 42: Software Development, IT & SaaS
  • Class 43: Restaurants, Hotels & Food Services
